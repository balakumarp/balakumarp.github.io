UPS® Network Carrier Mobile - Account Deletion Request

Users of UPS Network Carrier Mobile may request deletion of their application account and associated personal data by contacting the support team.

Request Email: UPSNCMPrivacy@ups.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number (that is used to login the app)
  • Reason for account deletion request

Upon verification of the request, UPS will process account deletion in accordance with applicable legal, regulatory, security, and business record retention requirements.

Certain information may be retained where required by law, legal obligation, audit requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security investigations, or other legitimate business purposes.

For additional information regarding the collection, use, retention, and protection of personal information, please refer to the UPS Privacy Policy.
